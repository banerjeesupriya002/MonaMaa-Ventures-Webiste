Cookie Policy

Detailed information about cookies and similar technologies that may be used on the Monamaa Ventures corporate website.

Effective date: 18 September 2026   |   Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar technologies stored on, or associated with, your browser or device. They can help websites remember preferences, maintain security, measure performance and understand how visitors use a website.

2. How we may use them

Our corporate Website is designed to work with a minimal technology footprint. Depending on the production configuration, we may use the following categories:

Strictly necessary

Technologies required for security, basic navigation, session handling or core functionality. These may operate without optional analytics consent where permitted by law.

Preferences

Technologies that remember choices such as display or language preferences, if such features are enabled.

Analytics

Technologies used to understand traffic, page performance and general usage patterns. These should only be enabled according to the final consent configuration and applicable law.

Marketing

No marketing or retargeting cookies are intended by default. If introduced later, the cookie inventory, notice and consent controls will be updated.

3. Current website implementation

The final cookie inventory should be kept aligned with the actual scripts, hosting provider, analytics tools, forms, embedded content and third-party services deployed on the Website. A policy should never claim that a tool is used when it is not actually installed.

4. Third-party links

Links to TrustRoute, Sonar Bangla Haat, app stores or other external services may lead to websites that use their own cookies and tracking technologies. Those technologies are controlled by the respective third parties and are governed by their own policies.

5. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to block, delete or restrict cookies through privacy settings. Blocking some cookies can affect the operation of websites that depend on them. Browser controls do not necessarily provide the same functionality as a website consent preference centre.

6. Consent and withdrawal

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies, we will seek consent before placing or activating those technologies and provide an appropriate mechanism to change or withdraw preferences. Withdrawal does not necessarily affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

7. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our technology stack or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.

8. Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, contact hello@monamaa.com.
