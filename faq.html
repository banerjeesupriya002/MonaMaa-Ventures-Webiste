What is Monamaa Ventures?

Monamaa Ventures is a venture-building and holding platform focused on creating useful businesses with a trust-led operating philosophy.

What businesses are part of Monamaa?

Our current portfolio includes TrustRoute, a verified carpooling platform, and Sonar Bangla Haat, a Bengali multi-vendor marketplace. Each venture operates through its own product and website.

Does Monamaa accept investment enquiries?

We welcome relevant investor and strategic partnership conversations. Any investment opportunity, if and when available, would be discussed separately and subject to applicable law and definitive documentation.

Can founders or businesses approach Monamaa?

Yes. We are open to thoughtful conversations with founders, operators, technology partners and businesses where there may be a strong strategic fit.
